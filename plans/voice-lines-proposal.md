# Voice lines: the starter set (proposed 2026-10-31, applied 2026-11-02, sharpened 2026-11-05)

Rules the set follows. One spoken sentence, or two that flow; no staccato fragments
("Phone. Down."), which text-to-speech reads as separate utterances with dead air between.
Four to twelve words. Commas where a person would breathe, not full stops. No "not this,
that" constructions. No lecture ("This is why you're behind"). Nothing that is only true
sometimes ("you're nearly there" at minute three is a lie). Slang only where it is stable
and the personality earns it. Each personality has one habit of speech that nobody else
has, so the four never blur. Back to work also plays when a break ends, so at least two
lines in each set mention nothing about closing anything. Every line must survive being
heard forty times.

Five Back to work (six for Mom and Gen Z), four Phone down, two Take a break, two Session done
per personality. One "that" per line at most, and only for the distraction. Stern is cold and
short, with no please. Mom threatens like a mom. Gen Z gets its spice where a line earns it.

## Gentle

Back to work
- Hey, that's not what we're working on.
- Come on back, you were doing well.
- Leave that for later, it'll keep.
- Okay, that's enough, back we go.
- Finish the work first, then you can have that.

Phone down
- Put the phone down for a bit, you can check it after.
- Phone away, you're in the middle of something.
- Leave the phone, it'll still be there in twenty minutes.
- I see the phone. Pop it face down and carry on.

Take a break
- That's a round done. Stand up, stretch, get some water.
- Nice one. Take five, you've earned it.

Session done
- And that's the session. Good work today.
- All done. Go do something nice with the rest of your day.

## Stern

Back to work
- That isn't the work. Close it.
- Close it. I won't ask again.
- You're wasting your own time. Back to it.
- You set this time aside. Use it.
- Enough. Back to work.

Phone down
- Put the phone down.
- The phone goes away until you're done.
- Phone down, eyes on the work.
- I can see the phone. Put it away. Now.

Take a break
- Round's over. Five minutes, then straight back.
- Break. Five minutes, not six.

Session done
- Done. Same time tomorrow.
- That's time. Good work.

## Gen Z

Back to work
- Bro, lock in.
- Nah, we're not doing that right now.
- Lowkey not the assignment.
- Be so for real right now. Close it.
- We're not gonna be bros if you keep this up. Close it.
- Caught in 4K. Close it.

Phone down
- Phone down, bestie.
- Bro, the phone. Put it down.
- The group chat can wait, trust.
- Phone again? Bro, we talked about this.

Take a break
- Round done, go get a snack or something.
- Okay that's a round, take five, you're cooking.

Session done
- Session done, you ate that.
- That's a wrap. Lowkey proud of you.

## Mom

Back to work
- I can see that from here, you know.
- Is that your work? No? Then why is it open?
- Don't make me come over there.
- Five more minutes of that becomes an hour. I know you.
- Close it, or you're grounded. Don't test me.
- I'm counting to three. One.

Phone down
- Phone down. I'm not asking twice.
- Who could you possibly be texting right now?
- Phone away, or it's mine till dinner.
- That phone again? I'm not angry, I'm disappointed.

Take a break
- Good job. Now go and drink some water.
- Well done. Take a little break, you've been sitting too long.

Session done
- All done? Good. I'm proud of you.
- That's finished? See, that wasn't so hard.

## What changing them costs

Derek and Elise ship with audio already made for each line, keyed by the line's id. New
text means new ids, new audio generated and bundled for both starter voices, and anyone's
recorded voice shows the new lines as not yet generated until they press Regenerate.
