The voice

A notification is easy to ignore. A voice isn't.

Record anyone for about twenty seconds and Supervisor builds a voice from it. The lines are generated once, on your computer, so a reminder plays the instant you need it.

Pick a personality for each session. Every line is text you can rewrite.

Gentle Mom Gen Z Stern

Elise · Gen Z

“Respectfully, get back to it.”

Derek · Stern

“That isn't the work. Close it.”

Derek and Elise come with the app. Your mom doesn't, until you record her.

The phone · optional

Closing the tab doesn't help if the phone is in your hand.

Turn the webcam on and Supervisor notices a phone in your hand. It only counts the phone when it can see one, so looking down at your desk is fine.

The camera stays off until you switch it on, and the app works without it. Frames are read and thrown away.

What it sees

There's no server behind this.

No account, no sign-in, nothing phoning home. Check the network tab on this page, too.

What it reads

  • The app in frontWhich program you're looking at.
  • The address of the page in frontFrom the browser's address bar, never the page. That's how it tells YouTube from Google Docs.
  • Whether the webcam sees a phoneOnly if you turn the webcam on. The verdict, never the picture.
  • SleepA closed lid counts as away.

What it never touches

  • What you type
  • Your screenNo screenshots, no scanning.
  • Page contents or browsing history
  • Your microphoneExcept while you record a voice.
  • Anything off this computerRecordings, lines, sessions and camera frames stay put.

All of it lives in one folder. Delete a voice, a session, or the whole history whenever you want.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11 · 64-bit

An installer, after the Mac build. New versions will show Windows' "protected your PC" note until the app earns a reputation; More info, then Run anyway.
